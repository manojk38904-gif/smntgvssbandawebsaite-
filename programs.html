Our Programmes

All programmes are implemented with community participation, local ownership and convergence with government schemes wherever applicable.

Cattle Welfare & Gaushala

Community Gaushala at Karbai Johari with fodder, water, shelter, hygiene and veterinary support for stray and abandoned cattle.

  • Establishment and management of community cattle shelters
  • Feeding, health monitoring, veterinary support and hygiene
  • Women volunteers in daily management
  • Organic manure production from the Gaushala

Women Empowerment & SHGs

Formation and strengthening of women's Self-Help Groups, leadership training, rights education and linkage to NRLM.

  • SHG formation with savings and credit access
  • Leadership training and rights education
  • Livelihood training for women
  • Linkage to NRLM and government schemes

Skill Development & Digital Literacy

A computer literacy centre for rural youth in Banda, plus vocational and employment-readiness training.

  • Computer literacy centre for rural youth
  • Foundational digital and internet skills
  • Vocational and livelihood skill building
  • Youth orientation and capacity building

Sustainable Agriculture

Support to small and marginal farmers through improved practices, input support, market linkage and farmer awareness.

  • Improved agricultural practices
  • Input support and market linkage
  • Farmer awareness programmes
  • Convergence with the Department of Agriculture

Organic & Natural Farming

Training in organic manure and Ghanjeevamrit preparation to improve soil health and reduce input costs.

  • Organic manure and Ghanjeevamrit preparation
  • Soil health improvement
  • Lower input costs for farmers
  • Awareness among 100+ farmers

Watershed & Water Conservation

Water harvesting, conservation and sustainable water management in drought-prone Bundelkhand.

  • Watershed development
  • Water harvesting structures
  • Conservation of water resources
  • Sustainable water management practices

Health, Nutrition & WASH

Health awareness camps, basic check-ups, hygiene promotion and nutrition education for women, children and the elderly.

  • Community health awareness camps
  • Nutrition education for women and children
  • Hygiene and sanitation promotion
  • Yoga and wellness camps

Plantation & Environment

Tree plantation drives, green-cover promotion and community-led environmental conservation.

  • Tree plantation drives
  • Green-cover promotion
  • Community-led conservation
  • Climate resilience awareness

Disaster Relief & Scheme Linkage

Food, hygiene and essential support during emergencies — including COVID-19 relief — and linking families to MGNREGA, PM Awas Yojana and other entitlements.

  • Emergency food and hygiene support
  • COVID-19 relief across Bundelkhand
  • Linkage to MGNREGA and PM Awas Yojana
  • Help with government entitlements
